About

Securing Digital Ethiopia

Abyssinia Sec was founded in 2023 by Ethiopian cybersecurity professionals who saw the growing need for structured vulnerability disclosure as Ethiopia's digital economy rapidly expanded.

As fintech, telecom, and government services go digital, the attack surface grows. We bridge the gap between the security research community and organizations that need protection.
